Why Does This Math Actually Work?

In the training corpus, the word king appears in contexts involving royalty and male references. man appears in male contexts without royalty.

Computing vector(king) - vector(man) numerically isolates the "royalty" direction vector. Adding this offset to vector(woman) shifts its position across the gender axis while maintaining royalty, landing right beside queen!

Module 4 • The Transformer Revolution

Contextual Embeddings: River Bank vs. Money Bank

Classical Word2Vec had a major limitation: every word had exactly one static vector. Modern LLMs use self-attention to dynamically re-project embeddings according to sentence context.

Sense A: River Bank (Nature)
"The fisherman sat on the muddy river bank with his rod."

Surrounding tokens (fisherman, muddy, river) attend to bank, pulling its embedding into the Nature / Water cluster.

Sense B: Money Bank (Finance)
"She deposited her paycheck at the local commercial bank."

Surrounding tokens (deposited, paycheck, commercial) attend to bank, pulling its embedding into the Finance / Commerce cluster.
